# Is the texture-inversion forward model physically adequate?

Literature review synthesised from three independent research sweeps (imaging/scattering theory, NDE polycrystal practice, ice-COF acoustics + FWI), 2026-07-29. Question posed after the inverted texture showed radial pie-wedge artefacts and a per-cell axis error that never beat the uniform global-axis baseline (~18-19 deg).

## Verdict on the forward model

NOT adequate for per-cell texture inversion, on four independent grounds, each tied to established literature. (1) Wrong model class for the task: the coda term (incoherent single-scattering sum of R^2 per range bin) is a member of the Thompson-Margetan/Rose independent-scatterer family (Margetan et al. 1994; Rose 1993), which the NDE community has only ever validated as a predictor of ENSEMBLE statistics (RMS grain noise, figure of merit, backscatter coefficient = Fourier transform of the two-point correlation of elastic constants). Backscatter from polycrystals is universally treated statistically, never inverted deterministically per grain: "a single signal is not adequate... a statistical quantity of the ensemble signals is used" (Zhang et al. 2020; Liu & Turner 2013; Insana et al. 1990 give the same verdict from quantitative ultrasound). Using one envelope per azimuth as a deterministic function of a specific boundary arrangement has no precedent and no theoretical support. (2) Wrong operator for the wavenumber band sought: envelope demodulation is documented as a LOW-wavenumber extraction operator, invented "for the recovery of low-wavenumber components of media (smooth background)" (Wu, Luo & Wu 2014). Discarding phase forfeits exactly the cross-range localization that circular-aperture reflectivity tomography relies on (Norton & Linzer 1979, 1981: coherent arc backprojection, PSF set by pulse shape). Incoherent R^2 is additionally sign-blind (+dv and -dv boundaries indistinguishable), enlarging the nullspace beyond what coherent backscatter theory already loses. So each azimuth constrains only "total boundary energy per range shell in the beam corridor", which the optimiser can trade freely within a shell, feeding the wedges. (3) Wrong scattering regime: with ~10 mm grains and lambda ~1.9 mm, ka ~ 16, the geometric regime, and "the strongly scattering geometric regime vanishes if the single-scattering Born approximation is employed" (Huang, Huthwaite, Rokhlin & Lowe 2022). The unfocused beam, ~100 mm two-way paths and late 24-36 us gate match the named breakdown conditions of the single-scattering assumption (Chiou et al. 2016; Hu & Turner 2015), and ignoring higher-order scattering biases inversions (Turner et al. 2025). Straight-ray ToF accumulation and c*t/2 depth binning are also invalid here: in coarse locally anisotropic media rays are "curved, complicated" (Kalkowski et al. 2023), and even the bulk velocity-averaging assumption for ice has been challenged (Maurel et al. 2016). (4) Amplitude budget: ice anisotropy is ~5 percent, "weak" (Gammon values via Gusmeroli et al. 2012), so the MAXIMUM single-boundary R is 0.0187, R^2 = -34.6 dB, and that only for maximally mismatched grains; with free gain, linear trend and floor parameters in the same fit, the validated window is plausibly dominated by the nuisance terms, not by boundary reflectivity. Finally, the parameterisation itself is unprecedented: 2.5 mm cells against ~10 mm grains is ~16 unknowns per grain, in a field where the best weld-tomography work deliberately coarsens to effective-orientation regions and uses ToF only, explicitly declining full-waveform amplitude data (Tant et al. 2020; Liu & Wirdelius 2014), and where the entire ice literature tops out at 14 ODF unknowns for a whole sample (Rathmann et al. 2022) with a 50-crystal statistical floor per velocity measurement (Kohnen & Gow 1979). The coda model is well matched to its role as an edge-density prior; the literature says it cannot be promoted to a per-cell orientation constraint.

## Verdict on the acquisition geometry

The observed failure is fundamental to the acquisition geometry, not a bug, and the user's limited-angular-coverage diagnosis is the textbook one, with one sharpening. What the theory says the diameters-only monostatic sweep measures: at each temporal frequency a monostatic plane-wave measurement samples exactly ONE object k-space point, F(-2 omega/c) on the beam axis (Kak & Slaney 1988, Ch. 8, eqs. 37-38); rotation plus bandwidth therefore covers only radial lines filling an annulus 2k_min <= |K| <= 2k_max, "a disk with a hole in the center", giving a high-pass image in which interior/average values are unrecoverable. At 2 MHz the finest sampled period is ~0.95 mm and placing 10 mm-period orientation structure inside the annulus would need usable energy near 0.19 MHz, a decade below band. Hence the piecewise-constant LEVEL content of the cell field (which cell has which axis) sits entirely in the hole and is supplied by the priors (TV, edge prior, global-axis anchor), which are the modern equivalent of Gerchberg-Papoulis spectral extrapolation, recognised as unstable (Roberts 1985; Kak & Slaney Ch. 8; Xu et al. 2004). What the geometry CAN determine: (i) high-|K| boundary-jump content, i.e. boundary geometry, and with COHERENT processing an actual reflectivity/boundary image via arc backprojection (Norton & Linzer 1979), a capability the envelope processing currently forfeits; (ii) bulk path-averaged anisotropy from ToF(az): but ToF(az)=ToF(az+180) for two-way diameters, so 360 azimuths give ~180 independent path integrals against ~1200 unknowns, and the ice literature finds azimuthal core sweeps saturate at ~12 azimuths for bulk CPO orientation and strength (Lutz et al. 2022), consistent with sonic logging inverting ONE scalar per depth (Gusmeroli et al. 2012). Per-cell view coverage: every off-centre cell is interrogated by essentially one beam direction, with effective angular aperture falling as ~1/r toward the rim (dx ~ lambda z/L, Schuster et al. 2017); microlocal theory says singularities without a matching measurement geometry are invisible and cannot be recovered "no matter how good the inversion algorithm" (Quinto 1993; Xu et al. 2004). The wedges themselves are predicted: the line-space data set is the measure-zero set p=0, so every measured line is a boundary line of the data set, and object-generated streaks along exactly those lines are provably the only singular artifacts adjoint-type methods add (Frikel & Quinto 2013; Borg et al. 2018), with direct experimental precedent in sparse circular-aperture ultrasonic reflectivity tomography (Amagai & Yamada 1995). The sharpening: 360-degree rotation covers all K-DIRECTIONS, so the wedges are not from missing directions; they arise from (a) one central spatial corridor per view smearing misfit along beams, and (b) the missing low-|K| annulus interior making per-cell values non-identifiable. Seismic practice states the same division: reflection data resolve only high wavenumbers; the low-wavenumber background must come from transmission-type data or a prior, and when it comes from a prior, accuracy saturates at the prior's accuracy (Mora 1989; Jannane et al. 1989), which is precisely the observed 18-19 degree plateau at the global-axis baseline. For calibration: even full coherent array coverage with EBSD truth achieves "average errors well below 20 deg" in weld orientation mapping (Kalkowski et al. 2023), and pulse-echo arrays degrade to 2-4x the through-transmission error (Singh et al. 2022); a single-element monostatic diameter scan sits below the bottom rung of that documented ladder.

## Missing physics, ranked

- Phase coherence (highest impact): cross-range localization in circular-aperture reflection tomography comes entirely from coherent phase across views (Norton & Linzer 1979, 1981); the 1-us incoherent envelope binning deletes it, and envelope data is by construction a low-wavenumber/smooth-background observable (Wu, Luo & Wu 2014). R^2 sign-blindness further enlarges the orientation nullspace.
- Multiple scattering and the geometric propagation regime: at ka ~ 16 the single-scattering Born model structurally cannot represent the physics ('the strongly scattering geometric regime vanishes if the single-scattering Born approximation is employed', Huang et al. 2022); unfocused beam, ~100 mm paths and the late gate are named breakdown conditions (Hu & Turner 2015; Chiou et al. 2016), and unmodelled energy is absorbed by the free gain/trend and projected onto the per-cell angles along beam directions.
- Ray bending and refraction: straight-diameter ToF accumulation and straight-ray c*t/2 boundary-depth binning misassign delay and depth coherently per beam direction; in coarse locally anisotropic media rays are curved and split (Kalkowski et al. 2023), and the underlying velocity-averaging assumption is questioned even for bulk ice (Maurel et al. 2016).
- Beam diffraction / finite Fresnel volume: no beam model at all, yet the first Fresnel zone at 2 MHz over this geometry is roughly 7-14 mm wide, 3-6x the 2.5 mm cell, so the 0.4-weighted lateral pairs operate below the transverse resolution of the physical beam (Hellmann et al. 2021; Schuster et al. 2017).
- Statistical nature of backscatter: a single-realization envelope constrains only two-point-correlation statistics of the gated volume (scattering strength, correlation length), not WHICH boundary at a given depth produced the energy (Zhang et al. 2020; Liu & Turner 2013; Insana et al. 1990).
- Missing low-wavenumber data channel: no transmission leg means the low-|K| interior of the annulus, where the per-cell orientation levels live, is structurally absent from the data and is being filled by prior-driven spectral extrapolation, which is known to be unstable (Kak & Slaney Ch. 8; Roberts 1985; Mora 1989; Xu et al. 2004).

## Recommendations

- ACQUISITION 1 (highest leverage): add pitch-catch CHORDS at several element separations on the existing 2 MHz rotational rig. A transmit-receive pair subtending angle phi samples |K| = 2k cos(phi/2), so sweeping chord separation from monostatic toward diametric walks samples radially inward through the missing annulus interior (Norton & Linzer 1979; Kak & Slaney Ch. 6; Devaney 1984). One extra receiver at a few fixed chord angles already multiplies the covered |K| bands, with no frequency change; per Virieux & Operto 2009, aperture diversity is the realistic low-wavenumber lever when low frequencies are impractical, which they are here (filling the hole by frequency alone would need ~0.19 MHz).
- ACQUISITION 2: add a transmission ToF channel at many lateral OFFSETS, not just through the centre. Transmission supplies the low-wavenumber background that reflection data structurally cannot (Mora 1989), converts ~180 central-path averages into a Radon-type sampling with genuine per-cell sensitivity, and is the configuration with the best demonstrated orientation accuracy (through-transmission MAE < 2 deg vs pulse-echo 2-4 deg, Singh et al. 2022). HARBUT (Huthwaite & Simonetti 2011) is the circular-aperture existence proof that ToF background + scattered-field detail spans both k-space regimes.
- ACQUISITION 3: keep the monostatic coda exactly in its current role, a high-|K| edge/boundary-density term. Theory supports that role and no other (Kak & Slaney Ch. 8; Norton & Linzer 1979).
- MODEL 1: re-parameterise the state to what the data can support: a low-order spatially varying fabric field, a handful of DOF per several-cm region or effective-orientation cells at or above grain scale, instead of 2.5 mm per-cell angles. This follows the entire precedent chain: 14 ODF unknowns per sample (Rathmann et al. 2022), deliberate coarsening in weld tomography (Tant et al. 2020; Liu & Wirdelius 2014), the 50-crystal reliability floor (Kohnen & Gow 1979), and the glaciology framing that acoustics constrains bulk anisotropy while thin sections give per-grain data (Diez & Eisen 2015).
- MODEL 2: reprocess the already-saved RF traces COHERENTLY. First deliverable: a Norton-Linzer arc-backprojection reflectivity image from the existing monostatic sweep, the one spatially resolved product this geometry can legitimately yield (a boundary map, not orientations). If moving toward waveform misfits later, plan for envelope/Wasserstein/frequency-differencing objectives because band-limited 2 MHz data with no low frequencies is cycle-skipping-limited (Rossato et al. 2026; Ali et al. 2025), but only after the acquisition upgrade; per Quinto 1993 no solver recovers what the geometry makes invisible.
- MODEL 3 (cheap and decisive): run the synthetic identifiability test with the existing validated FDTD (ppw>=6, order 8): simulate the exact 2 MHz monostatic sweep of a known 100-grain fabric and run the current inversion on it. If pie-wedges appear on perfect synthetic data, the failure is proven to be geometry/identifiability, not calibration, which is what the literature predicts (Van Pamel et al. 2017; Kalkowski et al. 2023 use grain-scale FE exactly this way).
- MODEL 4: audit the 24-36 us window before trusting any per-cell attribution: (a) estimate the single-vs-multiple scattering proportion, e.g. a Velichko 2020 style single-scattering-rate estimate; (b) check the window energy against the hard ceiling max R^2 = -34.6 dB per boundary implied by ice's ~5 percent anisotropy (Gammon via Gusmeroli et al. 2012), to test whether the fit is dominated by the free gain/trend/floor; (c) if the coda is retained as a statistical channel, add at least a double-scattering correction (Hu & Turner 2015).
- FRAMING: report the 18-19 deg plateau as a finding, not a failure. It is the theoretically expected information budget of a diameters-only monostatic acquisition (prior-saturation per Mora 1989/Jannane et al. 1989; identifiability limits per Yeoh 2026), and it matches the field's best full-aperture coherent result of 'average errors well below 20 deg' (Kalkowski et al. 2023). A negative/identifiability result plus the k-space design rule for the next scan is a publishable contribution consistent with the ice literature's expectations (Lutz et al. 2022; Rathmann et al. 2022).
